Privacy & Telemetry Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026. Safeguarding client confidentiality and vehicle data.

1. Information We Collect

Veloce Motors collects information necessary to deliver white-glove sales, ECU remapping, and telemetry calibration services. This includes customer identity details (name, email, telephone), vehicle VIN numbers, dyno diagnostic telemetry logs, and service history.

2. ECU Telemetry & Diagnostic Data

During dyno testing or track-side telemetry diagnostics, engine performance parameters (boost pressure, air-fuel ratios, torque curves) are recorded exclusively to optimize your vehicle's ECU maps. This data is never shared with third-party insurers without your explicit consent.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To process service reservations, vehicle acquisition inquiries, and parts shipments.
  • To dispatch 24/7 trackside emergency roadside assist.
  • To transmit product recall notifications and ECU software update advisories.
  • To maintain client portal records in your dashboard.

4. Security & Encryption Standards

We employ AES-256 bit encryption for all digital telemetry records and store client authentication credentials on isolated secure servers in full compliance with GDPR and CCPA standards.

5. Cookies & Website Analytics

Our website utilizes functional cookies to preserve your session state, remember dashboard navigation preferences, and measure web performance. You may disable cookies via your browser settings at any time.

6. Contact Data Protection Officer

For data inquiries, deletion requests, or privacy questions, contact our Data Security DPO at privacy@velocemotors.com.
